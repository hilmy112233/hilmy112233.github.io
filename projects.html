In the field: IoT & Embedded Hardware

FEATURED · CLIENT PROJECT · 2024

Smart Greenhouse Subang & Sumedang

The problem. Commercial fertigation controllers lacked APIs, manual mode switching required someone on-site to turn a knob, and fluctuating rural power caused system dropouts.

What I built. As project lead: an industrial electrical control panel with motor interlocks (CW/CCW), UPS fail-over, Raspberry Pi edge gateway with Python logging, and ESP32 sensor nodes (UV LTR390 & CO2) streaming to ThingsBoard IoT Cloud.

What changed. Deployed across five sites. Four of them reverted to manual operation — the hardwired manual path is what kept them running, which is the lesson I'd carry into v2.

ESP32 · FreeRTOS · Raspberry Pi · ThingsBoard · Panel Kelistrikan Industri · Python

FLAGSHIP · CAPSTONE S1 UNIKOM · 2025 – 2026

IoT Motorcycle Fuel Consumption & Telemetry

The problem. Carbureted motorcycles lacked precision fuel monitoring. Mechanical float sensors fluctuate wildly due to engine vibrations, potholes, and continuous fluid dynamic sloshing inside the tank.

What I built. ESP32 firmware with a 3-tier digital filter (7-sample Median Filter, Exponential Moving Average α=0.1, Hysteresis 0.03 L), 16-bit external ADC (ADS1115), NEO-6M GPS with Haversine distance tracking, and encrypted MQTT TLS uplink to Django & PostgreSQL.

What changed. Extracted stable, accurate fuel volume (Liters) and real-time efficiency (km/L) without sloshing noise, validated across actual urban driving conditions in Bandung.

ESP32 · C++ PlatformIO · ADS1115 · Median & EMA Filter · MQTT TLS · Django · PostgreSQL

EMBEDDED UI/UX · TEAM PROJECT

Flood & Water Tank — Panel & App Interface Design

The problem. Two water tanks and a flooding porch needed someone present at the right moment — and the two people living with it were never going to install, remember, and open a phone app.

What I designed. Two surfaces, one system. The 7-inch ESP32-P4 wall panel — two-button home menu, one-glance dashboard, status as word + colour + icon, a pump switch that shows its own state. And the mobile companion app: 25 Figma frames across authentication, home, device pairing and settings, sharing the panel's status vocabulary.

What changed. The panel shipped, is mounted, and runs daily. The app is fully designed and specified but not yet built. Firmware, sensor network, and enclosure belong to the rest of the Aligness team — both sets of screens are mine.

UI/UX Design · Information Architecture · LVGL · ESP32-P4 · Mobile App Design · Figma
